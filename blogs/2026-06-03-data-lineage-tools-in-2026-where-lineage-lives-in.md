The article argues that data lineage is not a single product category but rather a capability existing at different layers of the data stack - transformation, warehouse, observability, and catalog - each with distinct scopes. For teams requiring cross-tool visibility and AI enablement, catalog-layer lineage paired with context management provides the most architecturally coherent solution, unifying lineage with glossary, ownership, and classifications into a single governed graph.
